Gmail Configuration:
  1. Enable 2-Step Verification in your Google Account → Security.
  2. Navigate to App Passwords (search "App passwords" in the Google Account search bar).
  3. Create an app named "PowerGrid", copy the 16-character code, and paste it into the Password field above.
  4. Click Google Gmail preset, enter your full email, paste the app password, and click Save SMTP Configuration.
